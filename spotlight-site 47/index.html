FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will AI publish things without our approval?

Never. Every post, caption and budget change needs a human approval before it goes live. The approval step is built into the tool itself, so it is not a policy people are trusted to follow.

Which platforms do you work on?

Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Meta and Google Ads, plus trade press and social listening for PR.

Do you automate journalist pitching?

No. We automate monitoring and research only. Every pitch is written and sent by a person, because that protects your relationships with the press.

Will ad budgets move on their own?

No. Spotlight recommends changes, such as shifting spend from an ad set with three flat days of CTR. A person approves each move.
